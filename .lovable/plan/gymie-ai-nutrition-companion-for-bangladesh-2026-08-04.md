# Gymie — AI Nutrition Companion for Bangladesh

A conversational calorie and nutrition tracker. You type meals naturally in English or Bangla, the AI extracts foods, portions and full macros, logs them, and coaches you toward your goal (lean bulk first-class).

## Design direction

Framer/Apple-inspired: dark-first with light mode, glassmorphic cards, soft gradients, generous rounding, spring-based motion on cards, numbers and progress rings. Mobile-first, ChatGPT-style chat as the home screen. No purple-on-white generic AI look — a warm, energetic accent palette with deep charcoal surfaces.

## What gets built

### 1. Auth and onboarding
- Email/password + Google sign-in (Lovable Cloud), landing page for signed-out visitors.
- Multi-step onboarding: name, age, gender, height, weight, goal weight, activity level, gym frequency, daily steps, goal (Lean Bulk / Fat Loss / Maintain / Recomposition).
- Auto-computed BMI, BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor), TDEE, target calories, protein, fat, carbs, water. Editable afterwards in Settings.

### 2. Chat logging (home screen)
- Streaming AI chat. You type "Lunch e ek plate bhat, ek piece grilled chicken, dal ar cucumber salad kheyechi" and it replies with an itemized card: each food, serving size, kcal, protein, carbs, fat, fiber, sugar, sodium, plus a confidence badge (High / Medium / Estimated).
- The AI asks clarifying questions ("Was the chicken fried or grilled?") instead of guessing when it matters.
- Logged items are editable and deletable inline; totals update live.
- Understands Bangladeshi portion language: plate, half plate, cup, glass, bowl, piece, and dishes like kacchi, tehari, khichuri, panta, pitha, halim, bhuna, ilish, chingri, fuchka, singara, jhalmuri, mishti, borhani, cha.
- Quick-suggestion chips under the composer and a "your usual" one-tap relog of frequently eaten meals (AI memory).

### 3. Dashboard
- Today's rings and bars: calories, protein, carbs, fat, water, with remaining-calorie callout.
- Meal history timeline grouped by breakfast/lunch/dinner/snacks.
- Weight trend chart, weekly progress chart, gym status, daily streak.
- Water quick-add buttons.

### 4. AI coach and suggestions
- Contextual cards: "You're 600 kcal below goal", "Need 40 g more protein", "You haven't eaten since 2 PM", "Drink another 900 ml".
- Meal suggestions by time of day and remaining macros, biased to affordable Bangladeshi foods (breakfast/lunch/dinner/snack/pre- and post-workout).
- Natural-language weekly insight generated by AI from the week's logs.

### 5. Weight and progress
- Log weight; automatic weight graph, BMI graph, and lean-bulk progress against goal weight.

### 6. Weekly report
- Average calories and protein, calorie deficit/surplus, water, weight change, gym attendance, meal consistency, streak, plus the AI written summary.

### 7. Food data
- Curated seeded Bangladeshi food table (breakfast, lunch, street food, desserts, drinks) with per-serving macros, used as ground truth the AI matches against first.
- Unknown foods: AI estimates and the entry is explicitly flagged as estimated. Every AI-resolved food is cached back into the database so the library keeps growing.
- USDA FoodData Central lookup can be added later as a fallback; it needs an API key, so it is out of this build.

### Not in this build
Photo logging, barcode scanner, voice/Bengali speech, push notifications, family accounts, wearables. Reminder preferences are stored so notifications can be turned on later; in-app nudges cover the reminder behaviour for now.

## Technical notes

- Stack stays TanStack Start + React + TypeScript + Tailwind + shadcn/ui + Motion + Recharts (Next.js/Prisma are not applicable here; Lovable Cloud provides Postgres, auth, and storage).
- Lovable Cloud tables: `profiles` (biometrics, goals, computed targets), `foods` (curated + AI-cached, Bangla + English names), `meal_entries` and `food_items` (per-logged-food macros, confidence, estimated flag), `chat_messages`, `weight_logs`, `water_logs`, `gym_logs`, `daily_stats`. RLS scoped to `auth.uid()` on every table with explicit grants.
- AI runs server-side through Lovable AI with structured outputs: one extraction call returning a typed food list (with a clarification-question path), one coaching/insight call. Model: Gemini 3.6 Flash, streamed to the chat UI.
- Chat UI built from AI Elements; one continuous conversation per user, persisted in the database, with day separators rather than separate threads.
- Charts via Recharts, theming through semantic tokens in `src/styles.css` for full dark/light support.
